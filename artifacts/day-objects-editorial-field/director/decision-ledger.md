## Round 1 commissions

Two artists started from the same base `5a244de4ecc8c269e8f8abc34d8eb29fb38a2356` in isolated worktrees and could not inspect each other.

- Artist A explored asymmetric depth, continuous giant-to-tiny hierarchy, independent edge crops, and sparse counterpoints.
- Artist B explored relational zones, separated overlap chains, full-canvas small accents, and grammar-aware sparse tiles.

Both candidates preserved actor-local identity and passed the exhaustive anti-regularity corpus. Losing implementation branches were not merged.

## Critic loops

- Artist A was returned for: disconnected continuity, unreadable sparse tiles, an equal-scale diagonal bead row, and insufficiently distinct sparse scenes.
- Artist B was returned for: lower-knot overlap concentration, unreadable sparse tiles, and an `equalScaleStudy` that was not observably distinct.
- Each revision received only the largest gap and the next acceptance test. Critics were fresh and blind to artist reports, sibling packages, and prior verdicts.

## Selection

Artist A source `c6046f28347ed68034295968a26ff48ce19c629e` was selected.

The immutable package `26a3517f1fdbae1e32bf07855d6ee2c53b6144f26af9eff9472e9c3a97c6ca95` received two independent blind `PASS` verdicts without rerendering or source changes. Both critics verified the seal and inspected the complete breadth/continuity full-and-tile evidence plus debug overlays.

The full verified seal contained 149 MB of derived PNGs and stayed outside Git. The repository archives its contact sheets, canonical corpus copy, manifest, metrics, checksum inventory, and package hash under `artifacts/day-objects-editorial-field/composition/approved-c6046f2/`; this lightweight index is not itself a complete verifier input.

Observed accepted behavior includes:

- distinct, crop-safe one-to-three actor scenes;
- full-height asymmetric occupation and deliberate negative space;
- continuous scale/depth hierarchy with useful small actors;
- spatially separate cross-depth overlaps and independent crops;
- a rare irregular equal-scale study without a dominant row, ring, grid, or flower;
- exact retained actor geometry through the `1 → 2 → 3 → 5 → 7 → 10 → 5` continuity sequence.

## Freeze boundary

This decision freezes composition only. Material, palette, grain, motion, Metal parity, and production integration remain unapproved until their later gates pass. The main canvas remains unchanged; Day Objects stays Lab-only.
