# Day Objects — diversified complex gradients

Four ten-happening stills captured from the installed Debug app and the real
instanced Metal renderer in Day Objects Lab. The dates match the previously
reviewed complex-gradient examples.

- `01-two-colour-offset-03.png`: a wide two-colour sweep.
- `02-three-colour-offset-13.png`: warm three-colour roles.
- `03-three-colour-offset-18.png`: saturated three-colour roles.
- `04-three-colour-offset-23.png`: airy, lower-contrast three-colour roles.
- `contact-sheet.png`: all four examples at one glance.

The day owns one coherent palette grammar. Individual objects receive stable,
deterministic two- or three-colour subsets and orientations from that grammar,
so a scene stays related without assigning the same fill to every object.
Every transition is produced by broad overlapping radial fields whose centres
sit outside the carrier core. There are no linear or angular stops, hard colour
patches, centred targets, or colour rerolls during motion.

The captures use the debug-only arguments `-uiLab dayObjects`,
`-dayObjectsVisualHandoff`, and `-dayObjectsDayOffset <n>`. They do not alter the
main canvas or Gallery.
